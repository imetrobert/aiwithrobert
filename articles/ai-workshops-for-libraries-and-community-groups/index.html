AI workshops for libraries, residences and community groups

AI with Robert for Seniors offers friendly, hands-on introductions to AI for groups of older adults in Côte Saint-Luc and Greater Montréal.

What a workshop covers

A workshop is built around curiosity rather than technical knowledge. Participants try an AI assistant themselves, with guidance, on tasks such as writing a message, asking a question or exploring an idea. We also cover the limits of AI, why answers should be double-checked, and simple habits for staying safe online.

Who it suits

  • Public libraries and cultural centres
  • Seniors' residences and community associations
  • Clubs, associations and other community groups

A recent example

Robert led the workshop "Getting Started with AI: A Friendly Introduction for Seniors" at the Eleanor London Côte Saint-Luc Public Library. The library's Programming and Community Engagement Coordinator, Maria Ressina, shared her feedback in a public Google review.

Language and format

Workshops can run in English, in French, or in both. Group size, length and the room setup are agreed in advance, so the session suits your space and your members.
