What to expect from a private AI tutor

If you or a parent are curious about AI but unsure where to start, a private tutor can make the first steps feel easy. Here is what a typical start looks like.

It starts with a conversation

Before touching a device, we talk about what you would like to do: write emails, understand what ChatGPT is, feel safer online, or video call family. Your goals decide what we cover.

Your own device, your own pace

Lessons are one-on-one and can take place at home or online. If you have a phone, tablet or computer, we use it, so what you learn works on the device you will actually use. There is no rush, and we repeat things as often as you like.

Plain language, no jargon

Technical words are explained when they come up, or avoided. Questions are always welcome, including the ones you think are basic.

Practice on things that matter to you

We practise with real examples: a message to a grandchild, a question about an appointment, a recipe, a trip. You leave a session having done something, not just having watched it.

Honest about the limits

You will hear plainly what AI can and cannot do, why answers should be double-checked, and which information never to share. No tool or tutor can guarantee protection from every scam; good habits are what help most.

Small groups are available too

Some people prefer to learn alongside a friend or neighbour. Small group sessions are typically two to four people, six at most.

How to begin

The first step is a free 30-minute discovery call. We talk about what you want to learn, how you prefer to learn, and whether a session at home or online suits you better. There is no obligation.
