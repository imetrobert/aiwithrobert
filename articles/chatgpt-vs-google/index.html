ChatGPT vs Google: how they differ

Both help you find answers, but they work very differently. Knowing the difference tells you which one to reach for.

Google points you to sources

A search engine like Google shows a list of websites that match your words. You open the pages and judge them yourself. It is a good choice for current facts such as opening hours, news, prices, addresses and official information, because you can see where the answer comes from.

ChatGPT writes an answer for you

ChatGPT is a conversation. You ask in everyday words and it writes a reply, and you can keep asking follow-up questions, such as "say that more simply" or "give me an example". It is a good choice for explaining a topic, drafting a message, or brainstorming ideas.

Both can mislead, in different ways

  • With a search, some results are ads or low-quality pages. Look at who published the page.
  • With ChatGPT, an answer can sound sure and still be wrong, and it may not tell you where it came from.
  • Search engines now often show an AI-written summary at the top of results. Treat it the same way: useful, but worth checking.

A simple rule of thumb

  • Need a fact that can change (hours, prices, news, rules)? Start with a search and open an official source.
  • Need to understand or write something? Try ChatGPT, then check anything important.
  • Anything about health, money or the law? Use either to prepare your questions, then ask a qualified person.
